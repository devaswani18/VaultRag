Evalco Management Disciplinary Policy and PIP Protocol

1. Progressive Discipline Stages
Step 1: Informal verbal counseling by the direct manager.
Step 2: Written performance warning with HR concurrence.
Step 3: Formal 30-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
Step 4: Separation of employment if milestones are unmet.

2. PIP Execution Guidelines
The 30-day PIP document must specify at least three measurable deliverables. Progress must be reviewed weekly between the manager and HR business partner.

3. Immediate Termination Offenses
Gross misconduct including data theft, harassment, or willful violation of safety policies results in immediate termination without progressive stages.
