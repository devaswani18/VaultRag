# Evalco Executive and Manager Compensation Matrix

## 1. Annual Bonus Structure
Manager-level employees are eligible for an annual performance bonus with a target percentage of 25% of base salary. Executive bonus targets range from 40% to 60% based on company-wide EBITDA targets.

## 2. Salary Bands
- Engineering Manager: $165,000 - $195,000 baseline.
- Director of Operations: $210,000 - $250,000 baseline.
- Vice President: $260,000 - $320,000 baseline.

## 3. Equity and Stock Options
Management equity grants vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. Acceleration occurs only upon double-trigger change of control.

## 4. Severance Guidelines
Senior managers receive a minimum of 3 months base salary and executive team members receive 6 months in the event of non-fault restructuring.
