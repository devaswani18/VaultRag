# Evalco Summer Internship Guide

## 1. Welcome and Onboarding
Welcome to the Evalco internship program! Every intern is paired with a designated mentor. During week one, verify your GitHub Enterprise access and request an AWS sandbox account.

## 2. Daily Schedule and Standups
Interns participate in team standup daily at 10:00 AM UTC. Please share your daily progress, blockers, and planned tasks for the day.

## 3. Project Milestones
Mid-term project review occurs in week 6. Final presentations are delivered to the department directors during week 11.
