Acme Information Security Policy

1. Password and Authentication Requirements
All employees must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) on all company systems. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and changed every 90 days.

2. Device Security and Encryption
All corporate laptops and mobile devices accessing enterprise data must have full-disk BitLocker or FileVault encryption enabled. Screen lock must activate automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

3. Data Classification
Company data is classified into Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted. Customer PII and financial records are classified as Restricted and require encrypted transit and storage.
