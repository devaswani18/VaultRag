Evalco2 Corporation General Handbook

1. Headquarters and Facilities
Evalco2 corporate headquarters are located in Bangalore, India with satellite offices in London and Sydney.

2. Leave Entitlements
Evalco2 permanent staff receive 24 days of privileged annual leave and 12 days of casual leave per calendar year.

3. Working Hours
Standard office timings are 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM IST Monday to Friday. Flexible timing is supported with department lead sign-off.
