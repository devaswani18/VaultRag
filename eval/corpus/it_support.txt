Evalco IT Helpdesk and Hardware Provisioning

1. Hardware Refresh Cycle
Laptops are refreshed every 36 months. Standard issue hardware includes M3 Pro MacBooks for engineering and ThinkPad X1 Carbon for operations.

2. Ticket Submission
Open IT support requests by sending an email to helpdesk@evalco.com or messaging the #it-help Slack channel. Response SLA for standard tickets is 24 hours.

3. Peripheral Allowances
Remote staff receive a one-time home office ergonomic stipend of $500 for external monitors and keyboards.
