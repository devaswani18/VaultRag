Evalco Comprehensive Employee Benefits Guide

1. Retirement Savings (401k)
Evalco offers a 401(k) retirement plan through Fidelity Investments. The company provides a 100% dollar-for-dollar match on employee contributions up to 5% of annual salary.

2. Health and Dental
Full-time employees can choose between an HSA-compatible High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) or a standard PPO plan. Preventative dental cleanings are covered 100% twice per year.

3. Parental Leave
All eligible parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental bonding leave following the birth or adoption of a child.
