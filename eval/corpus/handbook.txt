Acme Corporation Employee Handbook

1. Working Hours and Remote Work
Standard business hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Monday through Friday. Full-time employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.

2. Annual Leave and Sick Leave
Full-time staff receive 20 days of paid annual vacation leave per calendar year. Sick leave is allocated at 10 paid days annually. Unused sick leave does not roll over.

3. Health Benefits and Insurance
Comprehensive health insurance coverage begins on the first day of the calendar month following employment commencement. Dental and vision coverage are included for employees and their legal dependents.

4. Expense Reimbursements
Business-related travel and meal expenses must be submitted within 30 days of occurrence via the Concur portal. Meal reimbursements are capped at 75 dollars per day for domestic travel.
